**Visit page and welcome**

- Removed the redundant Experience/instruction labels, Undated placeholder and repeated dish averages. Overall ratings retain their own meaning.
- Dishes have one Add action; tapping a dish opens ratings, with editing inside that sheet. Rating clearing is scoped to an individual person's draft.
- Photos have one Add action with camera/library choices. Captions and deletion are in photo options; full-image viewing supports paging and zoom.
- The initial terms acknowledgement is a friendly welcome with small Terms/Privacy links and one Agree and continue action. Account deletion and privacy controls live in Your account; reporting and blocking remain available from Board settings.
- Native preview used synthetic data and the actual visit components; it did not read/change real sessions, accept terms or create backend data. The actual simplified welcome was also inspected on Android without accepting it.
